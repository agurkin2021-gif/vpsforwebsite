Quick sizing answer

A practical starting point for WordPress VPS hosting is 2 GB RAM for a small well-cached site, 4 GB RAM for a business site, and 8 GB or more for heavier dynamic or multi-site workloads.

  • 2 GBSmall, well-cached WordPress
  • 4 GBBusiness WordPress with plugins and background jobs
  • 8 GB+Heavier dynamic load, larger database or several active sites
