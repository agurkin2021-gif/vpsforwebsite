Overview

VPSforWebsite.com is a public informational website. The site does not require an account to read its content.

Server logs

The hosting platform may process standard request information such as IP address, requested URL, user agent, response status and timestamp for security, delivery and operational diagnostics.

Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is enabled on the production website (measurement ID G-J6X9FNRMH5). Its script is loaded after the initial page rendering. Google may process visit, browser and device information and use cookies or similar identifiers according to the service configuration and Google’s privacy policy.

The site uses these measurements to understand aggregate site usage, not to verify the performance claims of any VPS provider.

Contact submissions

The site does not currently operate a public contact form or collect contact-form submissions.

Affiliate links and commercial relationship

The editor has a professional relationship with VDSina. Some links to VDSina are affiliate or partner links, and VPSforWebsite.com may receive a commission if a qualifying purchase is completed through one of those links.

Affiliate links are marked in the site code with rel="sponsored nofollow". The relationship and potential compensation do not change the workload-based sizing method, but they are disclosed so readers can evaluate commercial recommendations with the relevant context.

External websites

Following an external link takes you to a third-party website with its own privacy practices, terms and cookies. VPSforWebsite.com does not control those external services.

Cookies

The site does not intentionally enable a separate advertising cookie system. Google Analytics 4, the browser and the hosting platform may use cookies, technical storage or request processing to provide their functions. Browser controls can be used to manage supported cookies.

Data minimization

The site is built to avoid collecting information that is not needed for its core purpose: publishing VPS sizing guidance and directing users to provider offers they choose to visit.

Updates

This policy may change when analytics configuration, contact channels or other data-processing services change. The published policy should describe the services actually in use.
